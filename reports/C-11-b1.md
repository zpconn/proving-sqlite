The counterexample in every compare.f2i has i=0 and r bits
`11000011 11100000 00000000 00000000 00000000 00000000 00000000 00000000`,
i.e. 0xc3e0000000000000, exactly -0x1p63. sqlite3IsNaN's replacement returns
zero, consistent with its Proved contract. SQLite's guards at 92432-92433
admit this value. The failing property is
`sqlite3IntFloatCompare.overflow.1`, sqlite3.c:92434:

```
arithmetic overflow on float to signed integer type conversion in (i64)r
r > -9.223372e+18 && r < 9.223372e+18
```

The lower test is strict. C11 6.3.1.4p1 permits the conversion: the integral
part is exactly -9223372036854775808, representable in i64. This is a checker
false positive, not evidence of SQLite UB. It is consistent with a strict
lower threshold of INT64_MIN-1 rounding to INT64_MIN in double; that causal
explanation has not been verified against CBMC's implementation.
