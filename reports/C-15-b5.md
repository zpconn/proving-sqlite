**T-015 inclusion argument for review.** Let C be incoming capacity, A be aMem and S be sizeof(Mem). In the end-exact proof the shared allocation ends at A+C*S. At each loop header, the invariant gives pMem=A+u and 0<=u<C (positive C makes the u==0 alternative safe). Thus object_from(pMem) is [A+u*S,A+C*S), a subset of the function's [A,A+C*S) frame. It excludes the preceding UnpackedRecord. idx, d, u and pMem are function locals.

The start-exact proof's loop havoc also includes unused trailing backing bytes for C<4. Literal inclusion of that enlarged artificial havoc into the C-cell function frame is **not** claimed. The end-exact proof supplies the exact suffix inclusion argument; the actual source writes establish the same frame for the start-exact shape and larger caller allocations: loop stores address the current cell with u<C; SerialGet writes only u/flags/n/z, with its serialGet helper confined to u/flags; Unpack also writes enc/db/szMalloc. The final MemSetNull target is cell u-1 if u<C, otherwise cell C-1 after a break. Its u>0 guard prevents a negative cell index. Only default_rc and nField are written in the record. This is the source argument the independent reviewer must judge under T-015; the legacy engine does not check loop/function frame inclusion.

The other invariants keep pMem in aMem's object, idx>=1 and szHdr<=d, and the preceding cell non-dynamic when u>0.
