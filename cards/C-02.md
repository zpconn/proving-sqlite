- Notes: Pilot card. PROPERTIES.md does not exist yet, so this card states its property, from GOAL.md §2: "P2. Varints round-trip. For every u64 v: sqlite3PutVarint writes n bytes, 1 ≤ n ≤ 9, and nothing else. sqlite3GetVarint on those bytes returns n and v. getVarint32 returns n and v when v ≤ 0xffffffff, and n and 0xffffffff otherwise. sqlite3VarintLen(v) is n for v < 2^63 and 10 for v ≥ 2^63." Bring-up's native probe §1.8.1 measured exactly that for sqlite3VarintLen (9 at 2^63−1; 10 at 2^63 and 2^64−1; equal to n for every v < 2^63 tried): evidence/bringup/probes/varint_probe.out. Prove each clause for every u64 v on every configuration, with every option in proofs/CHECKS. "Nothing else" means no byte of the buffer beyond the first n changes. "On those bytes" means the decoders read only bytes sqlite3PutVarint wrote: show they do not read past p+n. Use the getVarint32 macro (upstream/sqlite3.c:22361) as SQLite does, since its one-byte case is inline. Appendix A facts: sqlite3GetVarint takes no length and reads up to 9 bytes, straight-line; sqlite3GetVarint32 reads p[1] and p[2], then calls sqlite3GetVarint for four or more bytes; putVarint64's loops run at most 9 times and sqlite3VarintLen's at most 10, so they may be unwound with unwinding assertions (builder §2.6). Do not add PROOF spans: these functions belong to other cards. Read proofs/HOWTO.md first: it records how proofs are built here.
